STEP 1: GOOGLE ANALYTICS 4 (GA4) SETUP — VISITOR & DOWNLOAD TRACKING
================================================================================
Why: See daily visitors, geographic regions, and track how many people click
     "Download APK".

How to set up:
1. Open https://analytics.google.com/ and sign in with your Google account.
2. Click "Start measuring" (or Admin -> Create Account / Create Property).
3. Account / Property Name: Vibentra
4. Reporting time zone: India (or your preferred time zone).
5. Currency: INR or USD.
6. Under "Choose a platform", select "Web".
7. Website URL:  https://vibentra-rgaq.vercel.app
8. Stream name:   Vibentra Web Portfolio
9. Click "Create stream".
10. Google will generate a Measurement ID formatted like:
    G-XXXXXXXXXX

How to add to website:
Once you have the ID, insert this code into the <head> of index.html:

<!-- Google Analytics (GA4) -->
<script async src="https://www.googletagmanager.com/gtag/js?id=G-XXXXXXXXXX"></script>
<script>
  window.dataLayer = window.dataLayer || [];
  function gtag(){dataLayer.push(arguments);}
  gtag('js', new Date());
  gtag('config', 'G-XXXXXXXXXX');
</script>
